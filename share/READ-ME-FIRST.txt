PURCHASE REQUEST HUB — PROTOTYPE
Woodcraft Rangers · FY2027

HOW TO OPEN IT
--------------
1. Click "PRF-Hub.html" in this folder, then click the Download button
   (top right). Google Drive cannot display the page itself, so it has
   to be downloaded first.
2. Open your Downloads folder and double-click PRF-Hub.html.
3. It opens in Edge or Chrome and is ready to use.

Nothing to install. No account, no internet connection needed after
the download. It is a single file that runs entirely in your browser.


WHAT TO TRY
-----------
- Click "Start a new request" on the home page.
- In the SITE box, start typing a school or department name, or a site
  code. All 204 active FY27 sites are there, grouped into
  DEPARTMENTS / OVERHEAD and SCHOOL SITES.
- Pick a site and watch FUNDING SOURCE fill in by itself.
- Add a line item or two; the grand total adds itself up.
- Sign at the bottom and submit, then switch the role dropdown in the
  top-right corner to "Finance" to review and approve it.
- With a request open as Finance, use "Download PRF PDF" to get the
  filled-in form as a PDF.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Sample requests 
are built in so there is something to look at. Any
  drafts you create are stored only in your own browser on your own
  computer, and are not sent anywhere.
- The site and funding lists are real FY27 accounting data. Everything
  else (vendors, amounts, approvals) is made-up sample content.
- This is a working prototype for feedback, not the live system.
